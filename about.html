About NEUROSTEP

Built around one question: what does this patient actually need to move again?

NEUROSTEP is a women-owned spinal cord injury and neuro rehabilitation center in Dehradun, built to bring hospital-grade recovery equipment — hydrotherapy, gait-training bars, electrotherapy — into one accessible, patient-first space.

Our story

Why NEUROSTEP exists

Most home-visit physiotherapy stalls at the same wall: the equipment that actually drives neuro recovery — a hydrotherapy pool, parallel gait-training bars, electrotherapy units — can't be carried into someone's living room. NEUROSTEP was built to remove that ceiling, giving patients with spinal cord injury, paralysis, stroke and other neurological conditions a dedicated, accessible place to do the work that home visits can't support.

What we stand for

Our values in practice

Patient-first pacing

Every plan is set by the patient's condition and stage of recovery — not a fixed program everyone is pushed through at the same speed.

Real equipment, real access

Hydrotherapy, gait-training bars and electrotherapy stay on-site so the tools that matter are always available — not scheduled around a home-visit calendar.

Honesty over hype

We don't promise guaranteed outcomes or invented statistics. We explain what a program involves and let patients make an informed decision.
