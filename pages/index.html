Why NEUROSTEP

Why recovery happens better here than at home

Four things set the NEUROSTEP model apart — and none of them are marketing claims, they're the reason the clinic exists.

Come to the clinic, not the other way round

Hydrotherapy pools, gait-training bars and electrotherapy units can't be packed into a home-visit bag. Real recovery equipment stays in one place — ours.

In-house hydrotherapy

Warm-water therapy that reduces load on healing joints and nerves while patients rebuild movement — a genuine differentiator most home-visit or clinic-only setups can't offer.

Built for accessibility

Wheelchair-accessible parking, entrance, seating and restroom, plus free on-site parking — designed for patients who can't afford friction on a hard day.

Personalised, not generic

Programs are built around each patient's condition and stage of recovery rather than a one-size-fits-all routine.

Signature strength

Hydrotherapy — the part of recovery that's hardest to get anywhere else

Water reduces gravity's load on a healing spinal cord or a recovering limb, letting patients move earlier and more confidently than they safely could on land. It's a core part of the NEUROSTEP program for spinal cord injury, stroke and orthopaedic recovery — not an add-on.
