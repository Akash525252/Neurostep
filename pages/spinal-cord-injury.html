In-clinic vs. home visits

Why in-clinic care beats home visits for spinal cord injury

Spinal cord injury recovery depends on repeated, weight-supported movement practice — the kind that needs a hydrotherapy pool, parallel gait-training bars and electrotherapy units. None of this travels. A home-visit therapist can offer general mobility exercises, but they can't recreate the controlled, equipment-supported environment where most functional gains actually happen.

That's the core reason NEUROSTEP is built as a destination clinic rather than a home-visit service: patients come to where the equipment already is, instead of waiting for equipment that can't come to them.

A typical recovery roadmap

Every patient's timeline is different. This roadmap describes the general phases of care, not a promised schedule or result.

01

Assessment

A detailed evaluation of current function, injury level and goals, used to design a starting program.

02

Foundation

Building core stability, range of motion and early movement patterns, often supported by hydrotherapy.

03

Progressive loading

Gradually increasing weight-bearing and gait-training work as strength and control improve.

04

Independence

Transitioning toward the patient's highest achievable level of independent function and home-management skills.
