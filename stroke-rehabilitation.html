Our approach

A hands-on approach to neurological recovery

Stroke and related neurological conditions affect movement, balance and coordination in ways that respond well to consistent, hands-on therapy. At NEUROSTEP, sessions combine manual neuro-physiotherapy with in-house hydrotherapy and gait-training equipment, so patients can practise real movement in a supported, lower-risk setting rather than relying on generic home exercises alone.

Programs are adjusted as function returns — starting with foundational balance and control work, and progressing toward independence in daily activities.

Works alongside therapy

Hydrotherapy for stroke & neuro recovery

Warm water reduces the effort needed to move, which helps patients recovering from stroke or a neurological event practise balance and coordination earlier and with more confidence than land-based exercise alone allows.
